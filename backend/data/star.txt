------------------------------------
SKILL: BPMN / PROCESS MODELLING
------------------------------------

EXAMPLE BPMN-01
Organisation: NSW eHealth
Period: 2022–2023
Context: NSW Ambulance IT Support (P1 / P2 incidents)

Situation:
NSW Ambulance and eHealth IT teams lacked a clear, shared understanding of how P1 and P2 incidents were handled, escalated, and reviewed.

Task:
Analyse the existing support process and define improved workflows aligned to operational needs.

Action:
Facilitated workshops with eHealth IT managers and NSW Ambulance stakeholders.
Created BPMN 2.0 AS-IS and TO-BE diagrams covering incident intake, escalation, resolution, and post-incident reporting.
Used BPMN diagrams as visual aids during workshops to validate understanding and drive agreement.

Result:
Improved clarity across teams, streamlined incident handling, and produced onboarding-ready documentation used by new team members.
